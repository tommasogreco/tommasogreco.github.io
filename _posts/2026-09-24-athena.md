## INTRO

Room URL: https://tryhackme.com/room/4th3n4

On a hot September afternoon, I thought I could relax by playing around with a machine available on TryHackMe. In the end, I decided to go with “Athena.” I must admit that I found every phase of the penetration test to be quite enjoyable and fascinating. Let’s go in action!

## ENUMERATION

Let’s start right away with a basic reconnaissance using “nmap” to look for open services:
![Nmap scan all port](https://tommasogreco.github.io/assets/img/blogs/athena/nmap1.webp)

Alright! nmap found the SSH port open, as well as the HTTP port, and ports 139 and 445 for SAMBA sharing. Entering Athena’s IP in the browser opens the website hosted on what seems to be a rather outdated version of Apache.
